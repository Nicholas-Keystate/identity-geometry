# The consumer register

The artifacts that instantiate this kernel, each with what it
instantiates and where it lives. The kernel grows only on a
consumer's need (the two-move discipline); this register is
therefore also the kernel's complete justification — every object
exists because something here needed it.

## 1. The Custos connectome

The claim registry over the ratified Custos 4.2 edition — 320
records charting the fold tower from the medium's mints up.
**First public artifact of the science.**

- Instantiates: all six kinds; the three-tier charting
  (medium / document-system / edition); computed positions;
  the de-facto-axiom census (30 evaluation points, 16 unheard
  at first projection).
- Location: the Custos repository, `connectome/` (registry +
  frozen schema v1 + projections). Shelved at v1 pending a
  prose-style ruling; the schema is the kernel's first frozen
  instantiation.

## 2. The conformance vector corpus

The Custos vector ledger (60+ entries, 14 families, case
convention) — operational identity geometry, built independently
by its author before the kernel had words for it.

- Instantiates: evaluation points (vectors ARE sampled
  coordinates); the mint/binding split (its direction-of-travel
  law — "a disagreement about an expected value is a finding
  against the specification, not a bug report against an
  engine" — IS the split); β-comparison grading (byte-grade
  same-engine, semantic-grade cross-engine until an encoding
  ratifies).
- Location: the Custos repository, `vectors/` (PR #72 lineage).
- Note: the third known instance of the ecosystem performing a
  mint before the science named it (SAID and CESR are the
  precedents).

vocabulary and cryptographically anchored content — label →

context compactions, constitution-sealed genesis, and committed

- Instantiates: the binding membrane (reader/agent bindings to a

  attributed via committed lens coordinates and judged against
  committed law — the lens is occupant-act attribution, never a
  fold-evaluator).
- Location: the anchor-mcp repository (CONTEXT-CUSTODY.md,
  CUSTOS-BINDING.md v4).

## 4. The lens question (a consumer of the evaluation law)

The governed standard's lens cites law content (composes from

- Location: Custos issue #74.

## Citation discipline

A consumer cites this kernel by revision (digest when the
repository's governance grade fixes one — see GOVERNANCE.md).
The kernel misreading a consumer's committed bytes is a kernel
defect, convictable against the consumer. A consumer drifting
from the kernel revision it pinned is the consumer's own
divergence.
