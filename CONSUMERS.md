# The consumer register — open by construction

The artifacts that instantiate this kernel, each with what it
instantiates and where it lives. The kernel grows only on a
consumer's need (the two-move discipline); this register is
therefore also the kernel's complete justification — every object
exists because something here needed it.

**The register is open.** These four are the consumers that exist;
they are not a roster of who may enter. Any artifact, community, or
working group that commits predicates over identifiers — or needs
to compare, chart, or sample them — instantiates this kernel by
citing a pinned revision, with no admission act and no permission
sought. A register that closed would contradict the science it
serves: recognition here is later and bilateral, never a gate.

## 1. The Custos connectome

The claim registry over the ratified Custos 4.2 edition — 320
records charting the fold tower from the medium's mints up.
**First public artifact of the science.**

- Instantiates: all six kinds; the three-tier charting
  (medium / document-system / edition); computed positions;
  the de-facto-axiom census (30 evaluation points, 16 unheard
  at first projection).
- Location: the Custos repository, `connectome/` (registry +
  frozen schema v1 + projections). Shelved at v1 pending a
  prose-style ruling; the schema is the kernel's first frozen
  instantiation.

## 2. The conformance vector corpus

The Custos vector ledger (60+ entries, 14 families, case
convention) — operational identity geometry, built independently
by its author before the kernel had words for it.

- Instantiates: evaluation points (vectors ARE sampled
  coordinates); the mint/binding split (its direction-of-travel
  law — "a disagreement about an expected value is a finding
  against the specification, not a bug report against an
  engine" — IS the split); β-comparison grading (byte-grade
  same-engine, semantic-grade cross-engine until an encoding
  ratifies).
- Location: the Custos repository, `vectors/` (PR #72 lineage).
- Note: the third known instance of the ecosystem performing a
  mint before the science named it (SAID and CESR are the
  precedents).

## 3. Context custody (the anchor harness)

A governed registry intermediating an AI agent's working
vocabulary and cryptographically anchored content — label →
SAID → payload, with seal-before-summarize custody across
context compactions, constitution-sealed genesis, and committed
rotation policy.

- Instantiates: the binding membrane (reader/agent bindings to a
  committed registry); occupancy beyond the human (an office
  whose accountability is structural, occupied by an agent
  loop); the proposer/checker seam in practice (model acts are
  attributed via committed lens coordinates and judged against
  committed law — the lens is occupant-act attribution, never a
  fold-evaluator).
- Location: the anchor-mcp repository (CONTEXT-CUSTODY.md,
  CUSTOS-BINDING.md v4).

## 4. The lens question (a consumer of the evaluation law)

The governed standard's lens cites law content (composes from
primitives) and an engine profile (composes from nothing). The
kernel's answer — the profile is a binding coordinate, not
content; era-graded citation, eternal inertness, vector-tested —
is the disposition of that standard's open primitive-closure
question, pending its authority's ruling.

- Location: Custos issue #74.

## Prospective consumers (named, not enrolled)

Communities whose existing work is already kernel-shaped, listed
so the door they would enter by is visible — named here as
prospects only; none has reviewed or adopted anything in this
repository, and nothing below implies otherwise.

- **ToIP governance working groups (Technology Stack / KSWG /
  the governance-framework community).** ToIP's governance
  frameworks are committed predicate-sets over identifiers by
  another name, and its "profile" vocabulary is the ecosystem's
  own word for what this kernel types as a binding. The governed
  standard's roadmap already carries the sponsoring-conversation
  item; when that conversation opens, this kernel is the
  vocabulary half of the offer — the frameworks they already
  write, typed so that conformance is computable.
- **The carriage-encoding design round.** Chartered, not yet
  convened; its decisions (clause denotation, shared predicates,
  transfer form) are mint-language work — the round would be the
  first consumer of the kernel's mint/denotation split in a
  standards-track setting.
- **Any second implementation of a governance evaluator.** The
  first engine binding beyond the reference fold makes the
  β-comparison machinery load-bearing for real; the corpus is
  already waiting for it.

## Citation discipline

A consumer cites this kernel by revision (digest when the
repository's governance grade fixes one — see GOVERNANCE.md).
The kernel misreading a consumer's committed bytes is a kernel
defect, convictable against the consumer. A consumer drifting
from the kernel revision it pinned is the consumer's own
divergence.
