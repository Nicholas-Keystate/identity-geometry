# Why this science — the justification

The kernel's README says what identity geometry is. This file says
why it is worth a repository: the claim, its evidence, and its
confessed grade.

## The claim

**Organizational mapping has been performing identity geometry
informally for as long as institutions have existed.** Every
tradition that maps an organization — charters, bylaws, org
charts, delegation-of-authority matrices, responsibility grids,
audit programs, compliance frameworks, governance frameworks —
has been committing predicates over identity-positions without a
medium that could hold the commitment. This kernel types what
those traditions were reaching for.

| The practice | What it is, typed |
|---|---|
| an org chart | a shadow-registry of offices: positions under law whose occupants pass |
| a bylaw / policy clause | a shadow-mint: a domain, a predicate, a sanction — stated in prose |
| a delegation-of-authority matrix | the enrichment ladder drawn as a table: who may commit what, under whose anchor |
| a committee charter | a seating covenant: powers enumerated, obligations attached, dissolution conditions |
| an audit program | a vector corpus: sampled evaluation points over committed policy |
| a responsibility grid (RACI and kin) | binding declarations: which office instantiates which predicate over which acts |
| "as-designed vs as-practiced" | binding divergence — the drift between the mint and its instantiation |
| an interim appointment | governed vacancy and caretaker law, performed by intuition |
| succession planning | the office's committed succession order, held in a drawer instead of an event log |

## The scholarly anchor

This is not a private observation. Institutional analysis found
the objects decades ago: the Crawford–Ostrom grammar of
institutions (1995) types every institutional statement as
ADICO — **A**ttributes (a domain: to whom the statement applies),
**D**eontic (may / must / must not), a**I**m (the predicated
act), **C**onditions (when and where), **O**r-else (the
sanction). An ADICO statement is a mint stated in prose: Σ in the
Attributes and Conditions, f in the Deontic and aIm, enforcement
in the Or-else. The grammar could classify institutions; it could
not commit them — evaluation stayed human, drift stayed
unconvictable, and the Or-else stayed exactly as strong as
whoever held it.

The office lineage is older still: the corporation sole (an
office as a legal person distinct from its occupant, so
succession never interrupts identity) and sede-vacante law (the
interregnum governed by rules fixed before the vacancy) are
centuries of jurisprudence about positions-under-law — enforced,
always, by courts and convention rather than by anything a
stranger could check.

## What was always missing

A medium. The mappings were descriptive because nothing could
hold a committed predicate over an identity-position in a form
any stranger could verify: the org chart drifted from the
organization silently; the bylaw meant whatever the board's
counsel said it meant; the audit sampled what the auditor chose
and testified on the auditor's authority. Precommitment theory
hit the same wall from the other side — the enforcement regress:
the promise enforced by the promisor. Every one of these is the
same missing substrate.

Key event logs and their governance layer supply exactly that
substrate: identifiers whose key state is replay-verifiable,
commitments anchored so that violation is convictable from bytes,
succession and vacancy as committed machinery rather than drawer
documents. The predicates organizational mapping always wrote in
prose become committable; the drift the org chart could never
confess becomes binding divergence, computable; the audit becomes
a vector corpus any stranger can re-run.

## The confessed grade

Two confessions bound the claim, per this repository's own
discipline:

1. **The claim is a program, not an achievement.** One standard
   instantiates the kernel end-to-end today, and its own
   implementability census records how much of it still rests on
   prose-denoted predicates. "Organizational mapping, typed and
   committable" is the direction; the current fragment is the
   kernel plus four consumers.
2. **The kernel survives the claim's deletion.** If the
   archetypal reading is wrong — if organizational practice turns
   out not to reduce to committed predicates over
   identity-positions — the six kinds and the composition law
   still stand on their four running consumers. The justification
   motivates; it does not ground. (Deletion test, applied to our
   own motivation.)

## What this makes possible, if the program holds

An organization whose chart cannot lie: offices as identifiers,
seatings as anchored covenants, authority as committed
delegation, vacancy as governed state, audit as replay. Not a
new theory of organizations — the old theory, finally with a
substrate that holds what it was always trying to say.
