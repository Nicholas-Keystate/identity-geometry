# The mathematical spine

The structure the objects generate when a whole corpus is charted.
Everything below the first section is CONJECTURAL at the stated
grade — the spine is offered as the shape the evidence currently
supports, with its falsifiers named. Nothing here is normative for
any consumer.

## The field (descriptive — this much is just honest bookkeeping)

A finding is indexed richer than a single value:

```
  F : Evidence-bundle × Law-sheet × Position × Clause × Frame → Codomain
        E                L           p          c         φ
```

"Different evidence is a different bundle; different law is a
different sheet" — the index names are quoted from the governed
standard's own engine-independence text. The full standing state
of a domain at a position is a FIELD over (clause × question ×
frame), evolving along the position axis. A conformance vector is
one sampled entry; a corpus is a sparse sampling; coverage is
subtensor density — which slices are heard, not just which points.

## Two ruled operations

- **Contraction.** Threshold machinery collapses an index with
  committed semantics: quorum predicates collapse the member
  index; compound results collapse the clause index. The
  contraction's refusal law: refusal propagates, findings
  preserve — refusal is never a value participating in the
  combination; it is a seam event that aborts it.
- **Transformation.** The frame-change law: given an edict of
  frame A and consumer B, committed rules state what the object
  becomes across the boundary. The three presentation forms are
  the three transport modes — replayed (recompute; the field
  pulls back), warranted (transport by committed morphism, a
  warrantor on the hook), proven (transport of the conclusion
  alone). The three form a resource-scaling ladder (replay
  burden vs proof size vs trust surface), measurable in
  engineering units.

## The gluing reading (conjectural, one falsifier from real)

Findings are computed per frame; an agreement law binds shared
sheets ("the same committed triple on the same sheet must agree —
for every conforming engine"). Local computations, a consistency
condition on overlaps, a global object defined by gluing:

- per-frame finding field = **local section**
- engine-independence agreement = **gluing condition**
- **duplicity = the obstruction to gluing** — two local sections
  refusing to agree on their overlap: a 1-cocycle that does not
  cobound. "Detects, never adjudicates" restates as: the medium
  computes the obstruction class; the tier above chooses the
  branch.
- the global section — the governance state of the whole
  ecosystem — is **a limit deliberately never taken**. What
  exists: the diagram (fragments + restriction maps over the
  scope order — a presheaf, where subset intuition is exactly
  true). The whole exists only as the universal property the
  fragments point at. The formalism independently refuses the
  God's-eye view.

**Second-order conjecture (H²-flavored):** if tier-2 folds resolve
duplicities locally (choose branches), whether those resolutions
themselves glue across a federation is a second-order obstruction
problem — the both-lawful race sits exactly at that seam, and the
composition tower may be cohomologically graded. Held behind the
falsifier below.

## The algebra check (honest failure + candidate repair)

Strict tensor talk fails: the codomain
{affirmed, defeated(cit), pending(typed), self-convicted(proof)}
is not a field, and its values carry witness decorations (every
entry has a derivation). Candidate repair: a TROPICAL reading —
under conjunction-of-requirements the order defeated < pending <
affirmed behaves as ⊗ = min; under alternatives ⊕ = max; weighted
thresholds evaluate as tropical polynomials (sum weights, compare,
step). If that holds, contraction is semiring-multilinear over a
proof-decorated tropical structure. CONJECTURE — unproven.

## The falsifier (named, first in line)

**Is warranted transport functorial?** A→B→C must equal A→C with
warranty composition, for the gluing reading and every
tensor-flavored claim above to survive. If composition fails
anywhere, the spine collapses to "indexed array with committed
rules" — and this file says so in its next revision rather than
softening the claim.
