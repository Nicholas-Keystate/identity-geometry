# Governance of this repository

**Grade at inception: UNGOVERNED DRAFT — deliberately, and
confessed rather than implied.**

This repository's founding faces a genuine tension between two of
its own program's laws, and rather than pretend the tension away,
this file states it and holds the decision where it belongs.

## The tension

- **The exemplar law** leans toward born-governed: the program's
  standards assert that domains ought to be constituted under
  committed founding law from birth (constitution-first genesis, a
  knot sealing founding bytes at inception). The sibling anchor
  registry did exactly this, and the reference-implementation
  defect that once made such seals silently droppable is fixed
  upstream — a knot anchored today actually carries. IG could be
  the program's first BORN-governed domain (the standards
  themselves are adopted-grade, their authority predating their
  law — a confessed lesser grade).
- **The gestation posture** leans toward waiting: the kernel's
  names are provisional (see NAMING.md), the spine is conjectural
  behind a named falsifier, and the coinage law says nothing binds
  before it has ripened in dialogue. Ceremonializing a gestating
  kernel risks anchoring words the science will retire — and a
  born-governed domain's founding law is the one thing it cannot
  quietly rewrite.

## The disposition

The decision is the ratifying authority's and is NOT made in this
file. Until it is made:

- The repository is a plain draft. No AID, no knot, no anchored
  acts. Its integrity claims are ordinary git history plus
  digests, and it claims nothing stronger.
- Nothing in it is normative for any consumer beyond the
  consumer's own citation of a pinned revision.
- When the authority rules, the lawful shapes are known: (a)
  born-governed inception — a founding law (C0-style: telos,
  amendment, refusal, naming-succession discipline) computed
  first, a new gAID whose inception seals its digest, this
  repository's record thereafter anchored; or (b) governance at
  first succession — the repository ratifies its first stable
  kernel revision by ceremony when the names settle, accepting
  adopted grade with the same honesty the standards did.

The one commitment made now, because it costs nothing and guards
the record: **this file's own history is append-only** — the
governance question's disposition will appear here as a dated
addition, never as a rewrite of this statement of the tension.
